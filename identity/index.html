What a Dash identity is

A Dash identity is a public account on Dash Platform, the data layer that Dash's evonodes run. It has an ID, a long string like 5DbLwAxGBzUzo…, and it can have usernames such as alice.dash.

  • Its keys prove an action is yours. A post, a poll, a vote, a push to a repository: each is signed with one of the identity's keys, and Platform checks the signature. Keys have a security level. The Master key changes the identity itself (its other keys), so no app should ever ask for it. Critical and High keys sign everyday actions; they are what apps ask for.
  • Its credits pay for what you save. Saving data on Platform costs a little. You top up an identity's credits with DASH: real DASH on Mainnet, free test DASH on Testnet.
  • It is yours alone. No dashhq app has an account server. Nobody, including dashhq, can reset a lost recovery phrase or move an identity for you.
  • It is public. Anyone can look an identity up in Explorer and see its usernames, its public keys, its balance and what it has saved. Its private keys never appear anywhere public.

One identity, separate sign-ins

  • The same identity works across the dashhq apps on its network. A Testnet identity works in Pollr, Yappr and Forge. A Mainnet identity works in Governance.
  • Each app signs you in on its own. Signing in to Pollr doesn't sign you in to Yappr. A passkey, a password or a limited key you set up in one app belongs to that app.
  • Testnet and Mainnet are separate worlds. A Testnet identity doesn't exist on Mainnet, and test DASH has no value. Testnet data may be reset.

  1. Address: Bridge makes a Testnet address and a recovery phrase. Write the phrase down.
  2. Testnet DASH: the Faucet sends free test DASH to that address.

Keep it safe

  • Never paste a Master key, or the recovery phrase of a wallet that holds real money, into a web page. No dashhq app needs either.
  • When an app asks for a recovery phrase, use one made for that app. Governance recommends this for its accounts.
  • On a shared computer, sign out when you finish. Pollr (with Remember me) and Yappr keep your key in this browser until you do.
  • A lost recovery phrase can't be reset. Passkeys and backup passwords only work in the app that made them; they don't recover the identity.
  • Check the address bar. dashhq apps live on dashhq.org (plus bridge.thepasta.org and yap.pr). A look-alike page asking for your phrase is not one of them.
